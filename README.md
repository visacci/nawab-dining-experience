# Nawab Dining Experience

# PROJECT: NAWAB ASIAN BISTRO & NAWAB GARDENS – PREMIUM RESTAURANT WEBSITE

Act as a world-class senior UI/UX designer, award-winning hospitality website designer, frontend architect and full-stack developer.

I want you to design and develop a complete, premium, visually stunning, modern and fully responsive website for **Nawab Asian Bistro & Nawab Gardens**, a restaurant brand based in Kampala, Uganda.

This must be a high-end hospitality website with the visual quality of an internationally recognised restaurant, luxury hotel or premium dining establishment.

Do not create a generic restaurant template. I want a unique, sophisticated, immersive and memorable digital experience that communicates exceptional food, hospitality, beautiful spaces and premium dining experiences.

The website must have beautiful photography, sophisticated layouts, smooth scroll animations, elegant transitions, interactive elements, excellent typography and carefully considered spacing.

The website should immediately communicate that Nawab offers two distinct dining destinations:

1. Nawab Asian Bistro – Acacia Mall, Kololo.
2. Nawab Gardens – 27 Bandali Rise, Bugolobi.

Both venues operate under the Nawab brand but offer different cuisines, atmospheres and dining experiences.
